Discover the Beauty of Coron, Palawan

Where Every Island Feels Like a Dream

Coron is consistently ranked among the most beautiful island destinations in the world — and for good reason. Towering limestone cliffs guard emerald lagoons, WWII shipwrecks rest beneath glass-clear water, and beaches stay soft, white and wonderfully uncrowded.

  • Swim in Kayangan Lake — famed as one of the cleanest lakes in Asia
  • Drift between the limestone walls of the breathtaking Twin Lagoon
  • Snorkel vibrant coral gardens and historic WWII shipwrecks
  • Meet giraffes and zebras at the one-of-a-kind Calauit Wildlife Sanctuary
  • End your day with glittering fireflies over the mangrove forest

Booking Notes, Terms & Conditions

Standard inclusions on all tours: Pick-up & drop-off (hotel), entrance fees, licensed tour guide, licensed tourist boat, life vest, lunch (where applicable), light snacks, and first aid kit.

  1. Rates are per person (php/Pax) and based on joiner/shared tour arrangements unless otherwise arranged.
  2. Pick-up times: Island tours A, B, Ultimate C & Super Ultimate — 8:00 AM-8:30 AM · Island Escape & Reef and Wrecks — 7:00 AM-8:00 AM · Calauit Safari A & B — 7:30 AM-8:30 AM · Coron Town Proper — 3:00 PM-3:30 PM · Glittering Firefly's — 5:30 PM-6:00 PM · Dugong Watching — 4:00 AM-5:00 AM. Please be ready at your hotel lobby within your pick-up window.
  3. Itineraries with "or" options (e.g., Atwayan Beach or Banol Beach, Skeleton Wreck or Malwaway Reef, Siete Picados or Las Islas de Coral) depend on weather, tide and coastguard advisories on the day of the tour.
  4. Weather policy: Tours may be rescheduled or rerouted for your safety if the coastguard suspends trips. We will always offer the best available alternative or a new date.
  5. Booking confirmation: Your reservation is confirmed once our team contacts you via your preferred method (Viber or WhatsApp) and payment arrangements are completed.
  6. Please advise us in advance of any food allergies, medical conditions or mobility concerns so we can prepare accordingly.
  7. Optional add-on: Bird Watching is available as an optional activity on the Dugong Watching tour.
